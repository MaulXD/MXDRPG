## Overview

This 16-page booklet contains **8 complete pregenerated character sheets** for the Starter Set, each spanning two pages: (1) the front stat sheet (double-column layout: Attributes/Skills/Combat Proficiencies on the left two-thirds, portrait + Endurance/Hope tracking + Conditions + Travelling/War Gear on the right third, War/Armour Gear tables along the bottom), and (2) a full-page portrait illustration plus an in-character quote and a short background paragraph.

**Characters captured, in booklet order:**
1. Drogo Baggins — Hobbit of the Shire
2. Esmeralda Took — Hobbit of the Shire
3. Lobelia Bracegirdle — Hobbit of the Shire
4. Paladin Took II — Hobbit of the Shire
5. Primula Brandybuck — Hobbit of the Shire
6. Rorimac Brandybuck — Hobbit of the Shire
7. Balin, Son of Fundin — Dwarf of Durin's Folk
8. Bilbo Baggins — Hobbit of the Shire

### Character sheet field structure (as observed — useful for validating the VTT's character sheet schema)

- **Header:** Name, Heroic Culture, Age, Distinctive Features (2, free text).
- **Three Attribute boxes** (STRENGTH / HEART / WITS), each showing:
  - A small "Rating" number (the raw Attribute score, top-right of the box).
  - A large "TN" number (Target Number, the prominently-displayed value actually rolled against).
  - A derived stat: **Endurance** (Strength column), **Hope** (Heart column), **Parry** (Wits column).
- **Skills:** 18 skills total, arranged in 3 columns of 6 under their governing Attribute (Strength: Awe, Athletics, Awareness, Hunting, Song, Craft — Heart: Enhearten, Travel, Insight, Healing, Courtesy, Battle — Wits: Persuade, Stealth, Scan, Explore, Riddle, Lore). Each has a checkbox (Favoured) and a diamond-pip rating track (0–6).
- **Combat Proficiencies:** Axes, Bows, Spears, Swords — each a diamond-pip rating track (0–6, or higher with gear).
- **Rewards:** free-text field listing special heirloom items (often blank on lower-tier pregens).
- **Valour** and **Wisdom:** single-diamond numeric fields (both usually start at 1, higher for veteran characters).
- **Virtues:** free-text list of special abilities with short mechanical effect descriptions (often noting "already counted in the total" when they passively raise a derived stat shown elsewhere on the sheet).
- **Current Endurance / Current Hope:** empty tracking diamonds (to mark off during play), each with small "Load" and "Fatigue" (Endurance) or equivalent reference labels.
- **Conditions:** Weary / Wounded checkboxes (unchecked on all 8 sample sheets — these are "fresh" starting characters).
- **Travelling Gear:** a named item plus the Skill it's associated with in parentheses (per the Useful Items rule in the Rules booklet).
- **War Gear table:** Name / Damage / Injury / Load / Notes columns (blank for unarmed Hobbits who own no listed weapon).
- **Armour / Helm / Shield table:** Name / Protection (or Parry, for Shield) / Load columns.
- **Portrait illustration** of the character (back page also repeats it full-page, with an in-character quote and a short third-person background blurb).

**Note on Rating vs TN:** the pattern observed across all 8 sheets is Attribute TN = 18 − Attribute Rating (e.g. Balin: Strength rating 5 → TN 13; Drogo: Heart rating 6 → TN 12). This is 2 points lower than the "TN = 20 − Attribute score" formula stated in the Starter Set *Rules* booklet (see `09-starter-set-regras-condensadas.md`, Characteristics chapter). The relationship was consistent across every character and every Attribute checked (both Hobbits and the Dwarf Balin), so it does not look like a one-off typo — it is flagged here as a discrepancy worth confirming against the Core Rulebook's actual pregenerated-character math before relying on either formula in the VTT implementation. Diamond-pip skill/proficiency ratings were read directly off the rendered sheet art; treat exact pip-counts as best-effort transcription and spot-check against the source PDF if a specific value is load-bearing for game logic.

## Cross-reference notes for the VTT character sheet schema

- All 7 Hobbit pregens share the "Hobbit of the Shire" Culture label and a similar Attribute spread (Strength rating 2–4, Heart rating 5–7, Wits rating 4–6) — consistent with Hobbits being emotionally resilient / physically unimposing per their Culture archetype.
- Balin (Dwarf of Durin's Folk) has a notably higher Strength rating (5) and Endurance (27), lower Hope (12), and is the only pregen with populated Rewards, War Gear, Armour, and Shield fields plus 3 Virtues and higher Valour/Wisdom (4/3) — consistent with being written as a "veteran" NPC-turned-companion rather than a fresh young Hobbit.
- Bilbo Baggins is similarly a "veteran" build: 3 Virtues, populated Rewards/War Gear, Valour 2 / Wisdom 3, and 6 Favoured Skills (well above the other Hobbits' 2–4) thanks to his Mastery Virtue.
- Every Virtue that increases a numeric derived stat explicitly says "already counted in the total" — confirms that on this sheet format, derived stats (Endurance/Hope/Parry) are always shown as final, post-Virtue values rather than base values with modifiers listed separately. The VTT's character sheet logic should likely store both the base formula result and any flat Virtue bonuses, but only ever display/use the summed total, matching this convention.
- Every "fresh" pregen (all except Balin and Bilbo) has zero Rewards and an empty War Gear/Armour/Shield table — confirming these fields are optional/nullable on the schema, not mandatory.
- The Travelling Gear field consistently pairs exactly one item with exactly one Skill in parentheses, confirming the 1-item-to-1-skill relationship described in the "Useful Items" rule in the Rules booklet.
